## Measurements needed

- PCB length, width, and maximum assembled depth
- Mounting-hole locations and diameters
- Camera lens center relative to PCB edges
- USB connector type and position
- Button diameter, thread length, and terminal clearance
- Existing wall opening and available mounting area
- Desired camera angle
- Exposure to rain and direct sun
